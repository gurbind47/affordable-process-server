What we serve and do

01

Process serving — all court documents

Small Claims, family, civil and Landlord and Tenant Board documents: claims, applications, motions, summonses, notices of eviction and more — served in full compliance with Ontario's Rules of Civil Procedure and Family Law Rules, with a status update to your office after every attempt.

02

Commissioned affidavit of service

Every serve comes with a sworn, court-ready affidavit of service at no extra charge — the proof the court needs, prepared the day service is completed and sent to your office the same day.

03

Court filing

We file your documents and affidavits directly at the courthouse — Brampton, Toronto, Milton, Newmarket, Oshawa and beyond — so your matter keeps moving without a second trip.

04

Rush and same-day service

Facing a filing deadline or an urgent motion? Rush service within 48 hours and same-day attempts across the GTA with prioritized routing. Tell us the deadline and we plan the serve around it — evenings and weekends included.

05

Skip tracing and locates

Respondent moved or avoiding service? We trace current addresses from lawful sources and document every attempt — the record you need if the court must approve substituted service.

06

Firm accounts and monthly invoicing

Volume rates for law firms and repeat counsel: one consolidated monthly invoice, itemized by file and matter number, with no per-serve paperwork. Set up an account once and send work by email as it comes.
