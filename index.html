What we serve and do

01

Process serving — all court documents

Small Claims, family, civil and Landlord and Tenant Board documents: claims, applications, motions, summonses, notices of eviction and more — served in accordance with Ontario's Rules of Civil Procedure and Family Law Rules, with a status update to your office after every attempt.

02

Sworn affidavit of service

Every serve comes with a sworn, court-ready affidavit of service at no extra charge — the proof the court needs, prepared the day service is completed and sent to your office the same day.

03

Court filing

We file your documents and affidavits directly at the courthouse — Brampton, Toronto, Milton, Newmarket, Oshawa and beyond — so your matter keeps moving without a second trip.

04

Rush and same-day service

Facing a filing deadline or an urgent motion? Rush service with a first attempt within 24 hours, and same-day service attempted the same day across the GTA. Tell us the deadline and we plan the serve around it — evenings and weekends included.

05

Firm accounts and monthly invoicing

One consolidated monthly invoice for law firms and repeat counsel, itemized by file and matter number, with no per-serve paperwork. Set up an account once and send work by email as it comes. Every attempt is documented, so the record is ready if you need to move for substituted service. Ask about volume rates for firm accounts.
